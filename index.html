I am a Ph.D. student in the Khoury College of Computer Sciences at Northeastern University. I am advised by Soheil Behnezhad.

I joined Khoury College in 2023, focusing on graph algorithms in sublinear models. Prior to my Ph.D., I worked as a research intern at ISTA, working on algorithms and computational geometry, and also worked as a research intern at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics, working on fairness in clustering. I got my bachelor's degree from Sharif University of Technology in Computer Engineering. I was awarded the gold medal in the 37th Iranian National Mathematical Olympiad in 2018.
